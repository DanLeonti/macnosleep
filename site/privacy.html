Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

MacNoSleep collects nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts and no network access of any kind. The app never contacts a server, including this one, after you have downloaded it.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings — whether a session is running, the dim delay and level, the auto-off duration, the battery floor — are written to the standard macOS preferences file for the app, under your own user account. Nothing else is stored, and nothing leaves your machine.

What the app can see

To do its job, MacNoSleep reads a small amount of local system state:

  • The battery charge level and whether you are on mains power, so it can pause when the battery runs low.
  • How long it has been since your last keyboard or trackpad input, so it knows when to dim the display. It reads the elapsed time only — never what you typed or where you clicked.
  • The current display brightness, so it can put it back where it found it.

All of this is read on your Mac, used immediately, and never recorded or transmitted.

This website

macnosleep.com is a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Cloudflare processes standard server request logs as part of serving and protecting the site; that is covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will change with it.
